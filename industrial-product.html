Production

Flour mill production

  • Preparing wheat: Weighing, inspecting, and grading wheat.
  • Cleaning: Removing dirt, stones, metals, and other seeds.
  • Tempering: Soaking wheat in water to remove the bran layer.
  • Gristing: Mixing different types of wheat to create a specific flour.
  • Milling: Breaking wheat into smaller pieces using rollers, then sifting to separate flour from bran and other coarse particles.
  • Blending: Combining different flours, such as white flour and wheat bran, to create new flours.
  • Enriching and fortifying: Adding vitamins and minerals as required by government regulations.
